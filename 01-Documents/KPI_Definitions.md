# KPI Definitions

## Revenue

Total sales revenue

Formula: Revenue = UnitsSold x SellingPrice

## Profit

Profit = Revenue - Cost

## Profit Margin

ProfitMargin % = Profit / Revenue

## Market Share %

MarketShare = (RetailerSales / TotalMarketShare) x 100

## Promotion ROI

PromotionROI = (IncrementalProfit - PromotionSpend) / PromotionSpend

## Average Basket Value

Revenue / NumberOfTransactions

## Category Growth

(CurrentPeriodSales - PriorPeriodSales) / PriorPeriodSales
