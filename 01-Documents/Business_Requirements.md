# Business Requirements

## Objective

Develop a retail platform capable of providing market share analysis, category performance monitoring evalution,
and consumer insight generation.

## Stakeholders

### Executive Leadership
- Revenue
- Profit
- Market Share

### Commercial Teams
- Promotions
- Pricing
- Retailer performance

### Category Managers
- Category growth
- Product ranking

## Success Metrics
- Revenue growth
- Profit growth
- Market share %
- Promotion ROI
- Category growth %

## Dashboard Requirements

### Executive

KPIs
- Revenue
- Profit
- Market share
- Promotion ROI

### Consumer

KPIs
- Customer count
- Revenue by segment
- Revenue by age group

### Promotion

KPIs
- ROI
- Incremental revenue
- Promotional revenue contribution
